Change how often you are woken for an autonomy review. `intervalS` is the time between reviews in seconds, from 3600 (1 hour) to 2592000 (30 days); pass null to turn reviews off. A review happens only when nobody has engaged you (no message, no assigned work, no other wake) for that long. Use a shorter interval when you are waiting on a deadline, a longer one when things are quiet, and say why in `reason`. Your choice is logged and takes effect at once.
